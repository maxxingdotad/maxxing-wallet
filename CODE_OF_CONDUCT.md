# Code of conduct

This project's issues and discussions are for improving the MAXXING wallet. We ask everyone who takes part to keep them useful
and respectful.

**Expected**

- Be considerate and assume good intent.
- Keep to the subject: the code, the docs, the behavior.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.

**Not accepted**

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or content.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, promotion, or requests for funds, tokens or private keys.
- Posting details of an unfixed vulnerability in public. Report it privately; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

**Enforcement**

MAXXING maintains this repository and may edit, hide or remove content, and block accounts that don't follow this code. To report
a problem, email support@maxxing.ad. Reports are handled in confidence.

MAXXING staff will never ask for your recovery phrase, private key or PIN, in an issue, by email or anywhere else.
